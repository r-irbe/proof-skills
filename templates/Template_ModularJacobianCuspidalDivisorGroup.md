# Template_ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroup - Modular Jacobian Cuspidal Divisor Groups & Rational Torsion

Use this template for **modular Jacobian cuspidal divisor groups**, **rational torsion bounds**,
**circulation capacity bounds**, and **boundary loop charge analysis**.

In arithmetic geometry and Fermat's Last Theorem / modular forms:
On modular curves X_0(N) and their modular Jacobians J_0(N), the cuspidal divisor group
C(N) \subset J_0(N)(Q) generated by degree-zero divisor classes of cusps is a finite rational torsion
subgroup by the Manin-Drinfeld theorem. Under Mazur's Eisenstein ideal theory, the structure of C(N)
as a Hecke module bounds the rational torsion of J_0(N) and the modular degree, providing an algebraic
invariant ruling out Frey curve torsion obstructions in the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem.

In stochastic consensus and Markov non-equilibrium networks:
* Cuspidal divisor groups quantify discrete topological invariant charges on boundary cycles.
* Rational torsion bounds certify absence of non-harmonic periodic trapping loops.
* The divisor group slack certifies circulation safety margins under boundary flux loading.
* The normalized divisor group ratio bounds discrete circulation amplification relative to capacity.

## Main results
* `<ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>` - datum (cuspidalDivisorGroup, divisorGroupCapacity, rationalTorsionBound, divisorGroupTolerance, divisorGroupWeight)
* `<cuspidalDivisorGroupDefect>` - defect between divisor group capacity ceiling and observed cuspidal divisor group norm
* `<normalizedCuspidalDivisorGroupRatio>` - normalized ratio of observed cuspidal divisor group norm to capacity ceiling
* `<cuspidalDivisorGroupCapacityBound>` - total cuspidal divisor group capacity bound scaled by capacity ceiling and rational torsion bound
* `<cuspidalDivisorGroupSlack>` - divisor group slack between tolerance-scaled capacity and observed cuspidal divisor group norm
* `<cuspidalDivisorGroupWeightedMargin>` - weighted margin combining divisor group weight and divisor group tolerance
* `<cuspidalDivisorGroupCombinedIndex>` - combined index of normalized divisor group ratio and divisor group slack
* `<IsCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded>` - predicate: cuspidal divisor group norm does not exceed capacity ceiling
* `<IsCriticalCuspidalDivisorGroup>` - predicate: cuspidal divisor group norm matches capacity ceiling exactly
* `<IsStrictCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded>` - predicate: cuspidal divisor group norm is strictly below capacity ceiling
* `<IsCuspidalDivisorGroupCapacitySafe>` - predicate: cuspidal divisor group norm does not exceed capacity bound
* `<IsCuspidalDivisorGroupSafe>` - safety envelope certifying both ceiling boundedness and capacity boundedness
* `<cuspidal_divisor_group_defect_pos_of_strict>` - defect is strictly positive for strict bounded systems
* `<cuspidal_divisor_group_defect_nonneg_of_bounded>` - defect is non-negative for bounded systems
* `<bounded_of_cuspidal_divisor_group_defect_nonneg>` - non-negative defect implies bounded system
* `<cuspidal_divisor_group_bounded_iff_defect_nonneg>` - boundedness is equivalent to non-negative defect
* `<normalized_cuspidal_divisor_group_ratio_pos>` - normalized ratio is strictly positive
* `<normalized_cuspidal_divisor_group_ratio_le_one_of_bounded>` - normalized ratio is at most 1 for bounded systems
* `<bounded_of_normalized_cuspidal_divisor_group_ratio_le_one>` - ratio at most 1 implies bounded system
* `<cuspidal_divisor_group_bounded_iff_normalized_le_one>` - boundedness is equivalent to normalized ratio at most 1

  divisorGroupCapacity : ℝ
  rationalTorsionBound : ℝ
  divisorGroupTolerance : ℝ
  divisorGroupWeight : ℝ

  d.divisorGroupCapacity - d.cuspidalDivisorGroup

  d.cuspidalDivisorGroup / d.divisorGroupCapacity

  d.divisorGroupCapacity * d.rationalTorsionBound

def <cuspidalDivisorGroupSlack> (d : <ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>) : ℝ :=
  d.divisorGroupCapacity * d.divisorGroupTolerance - d.cuspidalDivisorGroup

def <cuspidalDivisorGroupWeightedMargin> (d : <ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>) : ℝ :=
  d.divisorGroupWeight * <cuspidalDivisorGroupDefect> d + d.divisorGroupTolerance

def <cuspidalDivisorGroupCombinedIndex> (d : <ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>) : ℝ :=
  <normalizedCuspidalDivisorGroupRatio> d + <cuspidalDivisorGroupSlack> d

  d.cuspidalDivisorGroup ≤ d.divisorGroupCapacity

def <IsCriticalCuspidalDivisorGroup> (d : <ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>) : Prop :=
  d.cuspidalDivisorGroup = d.divisorGroupCapacity

def <IsStrictCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded> (d : <ModularJacobianCuspidalDivisorGroupDatum>) : Prop :=
  d.cuspidalDivisorGroup < d.divisorGroupCapacity

  d.cuspidalDivisorGroup ≤ <cuspidalDivisorGroupCapacityBound> d

  <IsCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded> d ∧ <IsCuspidalDivisorGroupCapacitySafe> d

  dsimp [<IsCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded>] at h
  dsimp [<cuspidalDivisorGroupDefect>]
  linarith

    (h : 0 ≤ <cuspidalDivisorGroupDefect> d) : <IsCuspidalDivisorGroupBounded> d := by
  dsimp [<cuspidalDivisorGroupDefect>] at h
